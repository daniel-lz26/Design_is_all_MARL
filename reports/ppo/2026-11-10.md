# PPO track, 2026-11-10

## What happened
- Built the reinforcement-learning trainer for the new cell simulation. One code path gives
  both IPPO (each robot learns on its own) and MAPPO (a shared training-time "coach" sees the
  whole cell). In both, each robot decides from its own view when it runs.
- Warm-started the robots by copying the best hand-written rule ("behaviour cloning"). The
  copy is as good as the rule: 49.5% vs 49.7% of orders on time.
- Ran a short training run (about 6 minutes). MAPPO improved on its starting point on all
  three training scenarios and already beats the best rule on them.
- On `main`: added scenarios that test the central scheduler losing its coordinator computer.
  Also confirmed that the auction baseline has no bug; it loses because free robots wait for
  busy robots that out-bid them.
