# PPO track, 2026-11-10

## What happened
- Built the reinforcement-learning trainer for the new cell simulation.
  - One code path gives both IPPO (each robot learns on its own) and MAPPO (a shared
    training-time "coach" sees the whole cell).
  - In both, each robot decides from its own view when it runs.
- Fixed the auction baseline on `main`: busy robots no longer bid (old version kept as
  `AUCTION_v0`). It is now the strongest simple baseline, 51.9% vs 49.7% for the best rule on
  `cell_random_10`. This needed observation v2 (tag `env-v1.2`).
- Added coordinator-outage scenarios (`env-v1.1`). One 30–90 s outage drops the central
  scheduler from 80% to 24% on time on `cell_10`; decentralized controllers are unaffected.
- Ran the six full PPO runs (IPPO and MAPPO, 3 seeds, 10M decisions each, 2–3.6 h each) and
  evaluated the best seed of each on every scenario.
- **Problem found: the learned robots abandon orders.** They deliver the orders that can still
  be on time, then sit idle and never deliver the rest. That inflates "on-time %" (it looks like
  64% vs the auction's 52%), but only about 70% of orders get delivered at all, and as few as
  38–51% on harder scenarios. The cause is the reward: giving up on a late order is almost free
  ("reward hacking", where the agent finds a loophole in what it is scored on).

## Results so far
On 100 test scenarios. "Delivered" is the share of orders that reach shipping at all; every
rule and the auction deliver 100%.

| Scenario | MAPPO on-time % | vs best rule | vs best auction | MAPPO delivered |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| cell_random_10 (random orders) | 64.2 | +14.5 | +12.3 | 71% |
| cell_random_10_disrupt (with breakdowns) | 47.4 | +20.7 | +21.1 | 51% |
| cell_10_disrupt (fixed orders, breakdowns) | 47.0 | +31.1 | +33.6 | 49% |
| cell_10 (held out, never trained on) | 60.0 | +10.0 | +40.0 | 70% |

**These on-time numbers are not a fair win** because of the abandoned orders. Full tables:
[results/summary.md](../../results/summary.md); per-seed results in
[results/ppo/seeds/](../../results/ppo/seeds/); details and the cause in
[docs/method_ppo.md](../../docs/method_ppo.md#results-full-runs-2026-11-10-not-valid-as-a-win-the-policies-abandon-orders).

## Blockers / decisions needed
- **How to stop order abandonment** (decision needed before retraining):
  1. PPO side only: don't let a robot park or wait while there is an order it could pick up, so
     every order is delivered and PPO just chooses the order. No env change; about 3.6 h to
     retrain all six.
  2. Env side (`main`, `env-v1.3`): change the reward so an undelivered order keeps costing a
     lot. Also affects the GP track.
- The GP track should check its policies' "delivered %" too; the same loophole is open to it.
