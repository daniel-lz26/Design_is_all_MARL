# PPO track, 2026-11-10

## What happened
- Built the reinforcement-learning trainer for the new cell simulation.
  - One code path gives both IPPO (each robot learns on its own) and MAPPO (a shared
    training-time "coach" sees the whole cell).
  - In both, each robot decides from its own view when it runs.
- Fixed the auction baseline on `main`. Robots that are busy no longer bid, so free robots stop
  waiting for them. The old version is kept as `AUCTION_v0`. The fix makes the auction the
  strongest simple baseline on most random scenarios: 51.9% vs 49.7% for the best rule on
  `cell_random_10`.
- The fix needed one new piece of information in each robot's view ("observation v2", tag
  `env-v1.2`). The PPO smoke checks were redone on v2, and both passed again:
  - Copying the best rule (behaviour cloning) gives 49.5% vs the rule's 49.7%.
  - A 6-minute training run improves on that start.
- Added scenarios where the central scheduler's coordinator computer goes down (`env-v1.1`).
  A single 30–90 s outage drops the central scheduler from 80% to 24% on time on `cell_10`;
  the decentralized controllers are unaffected.
- Team decisions:
  - Full runs of 10M decisions × 3 seeds for both PPO and GP.
  - `cell_10` stays out of training and is reported as held out.
  - GP moves to `env-v1.2`.
