## What happened
- **Fixed order abandonment on the PPO side (option 1 of the handoff, chosen by Daniel).**
  A robot may no longer park or wait while there is an order it could pick up. The rules and
  the auction already work this way, so the learned robots now have to deliver every order, and
  PPO only decides *which* order each robot takes next.
- **Set up the new PC** (Dell Precision 5860, Xeon w3-2423, 6 cores / 12 threads, Ubuntu).
  The baselines reproduce exactly here: 1,600 test episodes (BOTTLENECK/SPT, FIFO/EDD,
  AUCTION/SPT, AUCTION on the four main scenarios), 0 differences.
- **Smoke-tested** the fix, then **started the six full runs** (IPPO and MAPPO, seeds 0–2,
  10M decisions each) at 01:43.

  On `cell_10` the central scheduler wins clearly (80%) as long as its coordinator stays up; one
